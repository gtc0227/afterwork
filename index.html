On your own

Quiet ways to unwind and reset after a long day.

  • 🚶
    Evening walk in the parkFree
    A slow loop around the neighbourhood park or park connector. Leave the headphones off sometimes and just walk.
  • 🍳
    Cook one new dish
    Pick a recipe you have never tried and give it a go. Dinner and a small project in one.
  • 🏃
    Gym or a short run
    Thirty minutes is enough to shake off the workday and sleep better tonight.
  • 📖
    Read at a quiet cafe
    Bring a book, order something warm, and stay for a chapter or three.
  • 📷
    Photography walkFree
    Take the long way home and shoot the golden hour. Your phone camera is plenty.
  • 🧠
    Learn something small
    An online course, an instrument, a language app. Twenty minutes a day adds up fast.
  • 🚴
    Night cycling
    Cooler air, emptier paths, city lights. A completely different ride from the daytime.
  • 🎮
    Game night for one
    That game you bought but never finished. Tonight is the night.
  • 🗓
    Plan something to look forward to
    A weekend trip, a meal, a visit. Even sketching the plan lifts the whole week.
  • 🌌
    Do nothing, on purposeFree
    Sit on the balcony, watch the sky change, and let the day end slowly. Rest is an activity too.

With friends

Send the group chat a time and a place. That is the whole plan.

  • 🍜
    Hawker dinner crawl
    Everyone orders from a different stall and shares. Cheap, loud, perfect.
  • 🎲
    Board game cafe
    Two hours, one table, a game nobody has played before.
  • 🏀
    Badminton, futsal or basketball
    Book a court, split the cost, loser buys drinks.
  • 🎤
    Karaoke session
    Two hours of off-key anthems. Nobody remembers the score, everybody remembers the night.
  • 🎬
    Movie night
    At the cinema or at home with snacks. Let the group vote between two picks, no endless scrolling.
  • 🏪
    Night market wander
    Graze your way through the stalls and people-watch over dessert.
  • 🎣
    Prawning or fishing
    Slow, chatty, and strangely addictive. Dinner sorted if luck is on your side.
  • 🎳
    Bowling
    Works for any group size, any skill level, and there is always supper after.
  • 🍸
    Rooftop drinks
    One drink with a view. Go on a weekday when it is calm.
  • 🍽
    Home potluck
    Everyone brings one dish. Board games optional, second helpings guaranteed.
